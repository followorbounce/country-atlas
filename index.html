All land borders as a list

Borders and lengths: CIA World Factbook "Land boundaries" (January 2026 edition, its last), cross-checked against Wikipedia's list of countries by number of land borders; where the two disagree the Factbook figure is used and the choice is noted. Bridge- and tunnel-only links (Channel Tunnel, Øresund, Johor and King Fahd causeways) are not land borders and are not drawn. Positions are approximate centres (Google DSPL), nudged apart for legibility. Flags: flag-icons (MIT).

Growth Over Time

Indexed view: each line starts at 100 in its first available year, so trajectories are comparable regardless of the two entities' very different absolute scale. Hover a point for the actual value. Some entities/years have no data (border or currency-history reasons) and are simply skipped, not estimated.

Educational reference, not a live data feed. Figures are approximate, recent-year (2023–2025) estimates compiled from CIA World Factbook, IMF, World Bank, and UN sources, cross-checked where noted — see each metric's ⓘ for source and confidence, and the project's own documentation for what's in scope.
